# 💡 음악 프롬프트 예시 — 축제 프로젝트

> STEP 4(음악 생성) 프롬프트를 실행했을 때 나오는 결과물의 실제 예시입니다.  
> 핵심 씬 4곡의 예시를 보여줍니다.

---

## 🎶 예시 1: 오프닝 — 축제장 도착

> 감정: 설렘, 두근거림 / anticipation, excitement

### 음악 프롬프트

```
A bright and exciting morning adventure begins. A small character steps into a colorful festival for the first time. The music should feel like pure anticipation and wonder — like opening a gift on a special day. Start soft with a gentle ukulele melody, then gradually build with acoustic guitar fingerpicking, light tambourine taps, and a glockenspiel sparkling on top. The emotion is innocent excitement, a heart beating faster with every step closer to something magical. Warm, sunlit, full of hope.
```

### 스타일 태그

```
bright acoustic, ukulele, glockenspiel, fingerpicking guitar, gentle tambourine, children's animation opening, whimsical, hopeful morning, instrumental, warm and sunny
```

### 효과음 포인트
- 축제 깃발 펄럭이는 소리
- 멀리서 들리는 웅성거림
- 새소리, 바람 소리

### 설계 정보
| 항목 | 값 |
|------|-----|
| BPM | 100~110 |
| 장르 | Bright acoustic |
| 핵심 악기 | 우쿨렐레, 기타, 글로켄슈필 |
| 권장 길이 | 30~45초 |

## 🎶 예시 2: 함께 즐기기 — 하트 포즈 & 달리기

> 감정: 폭발적 에너지, "우리가 최고!" / unstoppable friendship energy

### 음악 프롬프트

```
Best friends running through a festival together — laughing, posing, having the time of their lives. This is the peak of pure fun. The music should feel like an explosion of joy. Bright brass fanfare announces "we're here and we're amazing!" Handclaps and tambourine drive an infectious rhythm. Strings play a fast, soaring melody full of energy. Everything is bright, loud, and unapologetically happy. The emotion is unstoppable friendship energy — two hearts beating as one, running side by side into the best adventure ever. This is their anthem.
```

### 스타일 태그

```
joyful orchestral pop, bright brass, handclaps, tambourine, soaring strings, buddy adventure anthem, festival energy, running together, children's movie highlight, triumphant, instrumental
```

### 효과음 포인트
- 하트 완성 시 "반짝✨" 효과음
- 달리는 발소리 "다다다닥"
- 깃발 펄럭이는 소리
- 캐릭터 웃음소리

### 설계 정보
| 항목 | 값 |
|------|-----|
| BPM | 125~135 |
| 장르 | Joyful orchestral pop |
| 핵심 악기 | 브라스, 핸드클랩, 탬버린, 스트링 |
| 권장 길이 | 45초~1분 |

## 🎶 예시 3: 석양 클라이맥스 — 풍선 구출 ⭐

> 감정: 극적 감동, 우정의 시험 / breathtaking emotional rescue  
> ⭐ **가장 중요한 곡 — 여러 번 생성하여 베스트를 선택하세요!**

### 음악 프롬프트

```
The most cinematic moment of the entire story. Sunset paints the sky in gold and pink. A small character floats up into the air, held by colorful balloons against the dramatic sky. Below, their best friend holds on with everything they have — refusing to let go. The music must capture this breathtaking, emotional climax. A solo cello begins — raw, vulnerable, trembling with effort. Then full strings enter, swelling with desperate determination. Piano joins with a simple but powerful friendship melody. The emotion is overwhelming — fear of losing someone you love, the stubborn refusal to give up, and the beautiful courage of a tiny friend holding on against impossible odds. When the rescue succeeds, the music releases into warm, relieved, tearful joy. Harp arpeggios shimmer like the last rays of sunset. This is the moment that makes the audience cry happy tears.
```

### 스타일 태그

```
emotional cinematic climax, solo cello, dramatic strings, piano, sunset scene, desperate rescue, friendship tested, swelling orchestra, harp arpeggios, tearful relief, children's film emotional peak, powerful and beautiful, instrumental
```

### 효과음 포인트
- 바람 "휘이이잉~" 소리
- 풍선 줄 "팽팽!" 당겨지는 소리
- 풍선 터지는 "펑!" 소리
- 착지 "쿵!" 소리
- 안도의 한숨 "후~"
- 주변 캐릭터들 박수 소리

### 설계 정보
| 항목 | 값 |
|------|-----|
| BPM | 80~90 |
| 장르 | Emotional cinematic |
| 핵심 악기 | 첼로 솔로, 풀 스트링, 피아노, 하프 |
| 권장 길이 | 1분~1분 30초 |
| 생성 권장 횟수 | 3~5회 ⭐ |

## 🎶 예시 4: 엔딩 — 벤치에서 마무리

> 감정: 잔잔한 여운, 조용한 행복 / quiet gratitude, lasting friendship

### 음악 프롬프트

```
The final scene. Two best friends sit side by side on a bench, watching a waterfall as the day comes to an end. String lights glow softly above them. Neither speaks — they don't need to. The music should feel like the quiet after a perfect day — full of gratitude, warmth, and the bittersweet beauty of a moment you wish could last forever. A solo piano plays a tender, simple melody — the friendship theme from earlier, but slower, gentler, like a lullaby. A cello hums underneath. Harp arpeggios drift in like falling leaves. The emotion is deep, quiet happiness — the kind that fills your chest and makes your eyes a little wet. As the music fades, only the sound of the waterfall remains. The best days always end too soon, but the memory stays forever.
```

### 스타일 태그

```
gentle piano ballad, solo cello, harp arpeggios, emotional ending, quiet gratitude, waterfall ambience, string lights, bittersweet beautiful, lasting friendship, children's film outro, fading to silence, intimate and warm, instrumental
```

### 효과음 포인트
- 폭포 물소리 "콸콸~" (지속, 자연스럽게)
- 바람소리 "살랑~"
- 나뭇잎 흔들리는 소리
- 전구 조명 미세한 소리
- 멀리서 새소리

### 설계 정보
| 항목 | 값 |
|------|-----|
| BPM | 70~80 |
| 장르 | Gentle piano ballad |
| 핵심 악기 | 피아노, 첼로, 하프 |
| 권장 길이 | 45초~1분 |
| 특이사항 | 마지막 10초 페이드아웃, 폭포 소리만 남김 |
